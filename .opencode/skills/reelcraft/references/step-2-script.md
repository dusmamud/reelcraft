# Step 2: Plan and Storyboard (The 4-Beat Retention Curve)

Vertical short-form video (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) has an unforgiving retention drop-off. 
If a video doesn't continuously reset the viewer's dopamine every 3–4 seconds, they swipe away.

## The 4-Beat Structure (Target: 18–24 Seconds)

```
[0.0s - 3.5s]  BEAT 1: The Hook (Pattern Interrupt)
[3.5s - 8.5s]  BEAT 2: The Agitation / Problem Setup
[8.5s - 16.5s] BEAT 3: The Tech Breakthrough / Visual Proof
[16.5s - 22s]  BEAT 4: Outro & The Comment/Follow Catalyst
```

---

### Beat 1: The Hook (0.0s – 3.5s)
- **Goal:** Stop the thumb from swiping.
- **Rule:** Big bold text on screen + High contrast badge.
- **Example:** 
  - Headline: "Chatbots Are Dead. Autonomous Agents Took Over."
  - Audio: Sudden bass drop + high impact whoosh.

### Beat 2: The Agitation / Problem (3.5s – 8.5s)
- **Goal:** Make the viewer feel the pain or realize an inefficiency.
- **Rule:** Contrast the painful old way with the new standard.
- **Example:**
  - Card: "In 2026, writing prompts by hand is a waste of time. While you're typing, autonomous pipelines are already shipping."

### Beat 3: The Tech Breakthrough (8.5s – 16.5s)
- **Goal:** Deliver the "Holy crap, that's cool" moment.
- **Rule:** This must feature tangible UI, code, or metrics.
- **Components:**
  - Glassmorphic metric cards (e.g. "10x Faster", "0 Config").
  - Code window with syntax highlighting and satisfying comments.
  - Kinetic typography with highlighted gradient words.

### Beat 4: The Viral Outro (16.5s – 22.0s)
- **Goal:** Convert view into a follower, star, or comment.
- **Rule:** Never end abruptly. Give a clear, simple call-to-action.
- **High-Converting CTAs:**
  - "Comment 'AGENT' and I'll DM you the repo link."
  - "Star the repo on GitHub • Link in bio."
  - "Follow for daily autonomous AI breakdowns."

---
