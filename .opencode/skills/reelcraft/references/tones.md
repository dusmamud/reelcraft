# Reelcraft Tone & Aesthetic Presets

Reelcraft supports 5 distinct visual and narrative tone presets. Each preset alters typography, color palettes, motion energy, and audio design.

---

## 1. `luminous` (Default — Modern Tech & AI)
- **Aesthetic:** Apple & Linear level polish. Frosted glassmorphism, Royal Blue glows (`#3b82f6`), soft radial backlights, crisp typography.
- **Font Stack:** Plus Jakarta Sans + JetBrains Mono
- **Motion:** Smooth spring transitions, subtle floating cards, high-contrast glow pulses.
- **Vibe:** Serious, premium, state-of-the-art. For real developer tools and products that aren't jokes.

---

## 2. `cyberpunk` (High Energy / Tech Rebellion)
- **Aesthetic:** Dark OLED black base (`#050508`), neon cyan (`#06b6d4`), electric magenta (`#ec4899`), subtle grid overlays, scanlines.
- **Font Stack:** Syne / Space Grotesk + Fira Code
- **Motion:** Snappy cuts, glitch accents, quick zoom-ins on code keywords.
- **Vibe:** Unapologetic, hacker-culture, cutting-edge AI breakthroughs.

---

## 3. `kinetic` (Fast-Paced Typography / TikTok Native)
- **Aesthetic:** High-contrast text pop, minimal backgrounds, bold words highlighted with vibrant pill highlights.
- **Font Stack:** Anton / Oswald / Inter ExtraBold
- **Motion:** 1-word / 2-word kinetic cascades, high word-per-minute delivery.
- **Vibe:** Maximum retention, fast scrolls, news updates, rapid-fire tips.

---

## 4. `apple-minimal` (Clean & Architectural)
- **Aesthetic:** Ultra-clean off-white or deep charcoal, razor-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`), generous whitespace.
- **Font Stack:** SF Pro Display / Inter
- **Motion:** Slow cinematic pans, precise cubic-bezier easing.
- **Vibe:** Thought leadership, architecture reviews, deep tech explainers.

---

## 5. `unhinged` (Silicon Valley Satire / Meme Energy)
- **Aesthetic:** Exaggerated VC metrics, fake $100M valuations, alert dialogs, red arrow callouts.
- **Font Stack:** Impact / Comic-Tech hybrid
- **Motion:** Shake animations, sudden zoom-cuts, dramatic pauses.
- **Vibe:** Humorous commentary on AI hype, absurdist startup launches.
