# Reelcraft Visual Tones & Design Presets

Reelcraft includes 6 battle-tested aesthetic design systems tailored for developer tools, deep tech news, open-source repos, and SaaS products.

---

## 1. `luminous` (Default for Tech News & AI Breakdowns)
- **Vibe:** Clean, editorial, authoritative, bleeding-edge.
- **Canvas Background:** `#F8FAFC` to `#EDF2F7` (Luminous Light Slate) with animated ambient cyber-grid.
- **Card Surfaces:** Frosted Glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`) with royal blue specular highlights.
- **Primary Accent:** `#1D4ED8` / `#2563EB` (Royal Blue) & `#0EA5E9` (Sky Blue).
- **Ambient Glows:** `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.18), transparent 70%)`.
- **Typography:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Display/Headings), `JetBrains Mono` (HUD badges).
- **Soundtrack:** Rhythmic tech groove / melodic synthwave.
- **SFX:** Heavy impact bell, crisp interface clicks, atmospheric whooshes.

---

## 2. `paper-and-ink` (SaaS Product Launches & Creative Audio)
- **Vibe:** Artisanal, tangible craftsmanship, warm studio elegance (as seen in StepGaana).
- **Canvas Background:** `#F2F2F0` (warm gallery neutral grey).
- **Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` (elevated card paper) & `#EAEAE7` (sunken wells).
- **Primary Accent:** `#8A6428` (Musical Instrument Bronze) with `#C9A468` (Bronze Lift).
- **Typography:** `Instrument Sans` (UI & headings), `Instrument Serif` (italic flourish), `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Visual Centerpiece:** Rotating vinyl disc with specular sheen, needle drop tonearm, live waveform bars.
- **Soundtrack:** Lo-fi jazz beats, acoustic rhythm, warm organic drums.
- **SFX:** Tactile needle drop, mechanical keypresses, soft UI mode switch.

---

## 3. `cyberpunk` (Autonomous Agents, Security, Deep Code)
- **Vibe:** Dark mode high-tech, futuristic telemetry, electric neon.
- **Canvas Background:** `#070A13` to `#0B0F19` (Obsidian Space).
- **Card Surfaces:** Semi-transparent dark slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`) with 1px luminous border.
- **Primary Accent:** `#38BDF8` (Electric Cyan) & `#8B5CF6` (Violet Neon).
- **Ambient Glows:** High-intensity radial blur glows (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`).
- **Typography:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Orbitron` with `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Soundtrack:** Heavy midtempo darksynth / bass drops.
- **SFX:** Low sub bass drop, digital telemetry clicks, robotic servo whooshes.

---

## 4. `terminal-hacker` (CLI Tools, Devops, Compilers)
- **Vibe:** Authentic IDE developer aesthetic, high readability, zero fluff.
- **Canvas Background:** `#0D1117` (GitHub Dark).
- **Card Surfaces:** `#161B22` (Terminal panel) with `#30363D` 1px border.
- **Primary Accent:** `#10B981` (Emerald Green) & `#38BDF8` (Terminal Blue).
- **Typography:** Exclusively `JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code`.
- **Visual Centerpiece:** Active terminal window with simulated prompt typing, colored syntax diffs, and execution badges.
- **Soundtrack:** Minimalist ambient lo-fi coding beat.
- **SFX:** Mechanical blue/brown switch keypresses, terminal enter chime, build success ping.

---

## 5. `apple-minimal` (Consumer Hardware & Polished Apps)
- **Vibe:** Ultra-clean, understated luxury, maximum negative space.
- **Canvas Background:** `#FAFAFA` (Pure Studio Light) or `#000000` (Pure OLED Dark).
- **Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` with ultra-soft 40px blur shadow (`0 20px 50px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).
- **Primary Accent:** `#000000` (Ink on light) or `#0071E3` (Apple Royal Blue).
- **Typography:** `SF Pro Display` / `Inter` (Tight letter-spacing `-0.03em`).
- **Soundtrack:** Gentle piano acoustic swell or contemporary cinematic pulses.
- **SFX:** Soft wooden pops, gentle air whooshes.

---

## 6. `unhinged-viral` (Memes, Satire, High-Energy Shits & Giggles)
- **Vibe:** Aggressive pattern interrupt, bold contrasting stickers, fast velocity.
- **Canvas Background:** High-contrast `#09090B` with caution stripes or vibrant electric yellow (`#FACC15`).
- **Card Surfaces:** High-contrast border badges (`border: 3px solid #FACC15`).
- **Typography:** Ultra-heavy weights (`Impact`, `Syne`, or 900-weight sans).
- **Soundtrack:** Fast hyperpop / 140+ BPM breakbeats.
- **SFX:** Explosive bass booms, meme buzzer, warp speed whooshes.
