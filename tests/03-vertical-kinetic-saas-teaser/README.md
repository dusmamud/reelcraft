# StepGaana 20s Fast-Paced Kinetic Product Teaser

This folder contains a 20-second vertical (9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps) high-retention kinetic product teaser for **StepGaana**, engineered with rapid-fire typographic transitions, beat-synced card animations, and warm vinyl aesthetics.

---

## 1. Specifications & Assets
- **Format:** 9:16 Vertical (1080x1920)
- **Duration:** 20.0 seconds
- **Frame Rate:** 30 fps (60 fps renderable)
- **Video Output:** `video.mp4` (5.9 MB, rendered via HyperFrames)
- **Poster Thumbnail:** `poster.jpg` (Frame 1s high-res snapshot)
- **Soundtrack:** `assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- **SFX:** Synchronized mechanical switches, interface clicks, and heavy impact bell

---

## 2. Kinetic Sequence Timeline
- **0.0s – 3.5s (Hook):** Dark vinyl silhouette reveal with headline *"What if you could turn words into music?"*
- **3.5s – 8.0s (Mode Swaps):** Instantaneous arrival of 4 core studio chips (Song Creation, Instrumentals, Vocals, Covers).
- **8.0s – 14.5s (Live Playback):** Needle drops on vinyl record, dynamic waveform surges in bronze-gold glow.
- **14.5s – 20.0s (Outro & CTA):** Fast logo arrival, creator watermark, and crisp *"Start Creating Today · stepgaana.app"* call-to-action.
