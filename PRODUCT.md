# Product Philosophy: Reelcraft

## Brand Identity

**Name:** Reelcraft  
**Tagline:** You built the code. Now make it go viral.  
**Personality:** Cinematic, High Energy, Technically Rigorous, No-Nonsense.

---

## The Target User

1. **Developers & Indie Hackers:** People building software who want to showcase what they shipped on Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts without spending 4 hours in Premiere Pro or CapCut.
2. **AI Builders & Tech Founders:** Creators launching autonomous agents, CLI tools, libraries, or developer platforms who need high-converting short-form video to drive GitHub stars and early adopters.
3. **Tech Influencers & Educators:** Developers posting daily tips, code snippets, and architecture breakdowns who need a consistent, high-fidelity visual engine.

---

## Core Product Purpose

Reelcraft exists to turn code, READMEs, and technical architectures into high-retention 9:16 vertical videos in 30 seconds.

It achieves this by:
1. Automatically extracting the single most scroll-stopping hook from the codebase.
2. Formulating a 4-beat retention storyboard designed for social media algorithms.
3. Rendering 1080x1920 60fps kinetic HTML/CSS/JS compositions via Hyperframes.
4. Packaging the render with frame-0 thumbnails, sound effects, and postable captions.

---

## Anti-Patterns (What Reelcraft Must NEVER Be)

- **No Boring PowerPoint Slides:** We do not generate static bullet points fading in and out.
- **No Cheesy Stock Footage:** We do not overlay random stock clips of business people typing on laptops.
- **No Broken Aspect Ratios:** We never letterbox landscape 16:9 videos into vertical formats. Every layout is built natively for 9:16.
- **No Safe-Zone Violations:** Text will never be hidden behind Instagram's right-side icons (like, comment, share) or bottom audio tickers.
- **No Abstract Corporate Fluff:** We never use "Elevate your synergy" or "Streamline your workflows." We showcase actual code, real terminal logs, and concrete metrics.

---

## Creative Golden Rules

1. **The 2.5-Second Threshold:** If the hook doesn't create curiosity or disrupt the viewer's scroll within 2.5 seconds, the reel fails. Plan the hook before anything else.
2. **Kinetic Typography:** Words should feel alive. Use snappy spring cubic-beziers (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). Fast-in, hold to read, then clean exit.
3. **Sound-Driven Motion:** Every significant visual change must have an accompanying sound cue (impact on hook, whoosh on scene transition, pop on badges).
4. **Platform Native:** The output is designed specifically for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok.
