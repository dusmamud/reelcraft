# 12 Proven Viral Hook Archetypes for Tech & Developer Reels

The first 2.5 seconds dictate whether your video reaches 1,000 views or 1,000,000 views. Choose 1 archetype from this playbook to anchor Beat 1:

---

### 1. The Paradigm Shift (Most Viral)
- **Formula:** *"[Current standard] is officially dead. In 2026, [New paradigm] took over."*
- **Example:** "Chatbots are officially dead. In 2026, autonomous agent teams took over."
- **Visual:** Red warning badge + strike-through text on 2024 tech.

### 2. The Extreme Contrast
- **Formula:** *"This [X]-line open source script replaces an entire $[Y]/mo platform."*
- **Example:** "This 200-line script replaces an entire $5,000 a month SaaS platform."
- **Visual:** Struck-through price tag ($5,000/mo) slammed down to $0.

### 3. The Urgent Warning
- **Formula:** *"Stop [common developer action] by hand in 2026. Do this instead."*
- **Example:** "Stop writing video motion scripts by hand in 2026. Do this instead."
- **Visual:** Warning ticker + animated blinking cursor.

### 4. The Provocative Wonder Question
- **Formula:** *"What if you could [do incredible action] in [tiny duration]?"*
- **Example:** "What if you could turn words into full studio-mastered music?"
- **Visual:** Stylized spinning vinyl disc with glowing bronze accents.

### 5. The Silicon Valley "Secret"
- **Formula:** *"Big tech companies don't want you to know about this free repo."*
- **Example:** "Big tech companies don't want you to know about this local TTS model."
- **Visual:** Redacted document / classified HUD badge.

### 6. The Speed Demon
- **Formula:** *"[Massive technical task] generated and deployed in [X] seconds flat."*
- **Example:** "A 60fps kinetic motion video generated from raw code in 4.2 seconds flat."
- **Visual:** Live millisecond digital stopwatch ticking up rapidly.

### 7. The Free vs Expensive David-and-Goliath
- **Formula:** *"This free open-source model just outperformed a $20/month subscription."*
- **Example:** "This local 82M open-source model just beat commercial cloud voices."
- **Visual:** Side-by-side benchmark comparison card with green checkmark.

### 8. The Future Shock
- **Formula:** *"The future arrived sooner than we expected. Look at this."*
- **Example:** "Physical AI and humanoid robotics just reached sub-millimeter precision."
- **Visual:** Photorealistic humanoid robotics visual with Ken Burns cinematic zoom.

### 9. The Developer "Cheat Code"
- **Formula:** *"I built the ultimate developer cheat code for [niche task]."*
- **Example:** "I built the ultimate developer cheat code for turning GitHub repos into viral TikToks."
- **Visual:** Terminal window typing `$ npx reelcraft --viral`.

### 10. The Reverse Psychology
- **Formula:** *"Whatever you do, don't use this tool unless you want [huge benefit]."*
- **Example:** "Whatever you do, don't use this CLI unless you want your code to go viral."
- **Visual:** Caution hazard icon flashing at 0.2s.

### 11. The Metric Flex
- **Formula:** *"From zero to [impressive metric] in [unbelievable timeframe]."*
- **Example:** "From zero to 60 frames per second 1080p render in a single command."
- **Visual:** Large stat counter slam filling the center viewport.

### 12. The Direct Challenge
- **Formula:** *"Are you still [doing slow thing]? Why haven't you switched to [new tech]?"*
- **Example:** "Are you still recording screen videos manually? Why haven't you switched to code-driven video?"
- **Visual:** Split screen showing frantic manual recording vs instantaneous one-line command.
